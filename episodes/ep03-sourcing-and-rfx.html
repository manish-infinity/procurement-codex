Episode 03 · Going to market

Sourcing & RFx

A category strategy is only a plan until you take it to the market. Sourcing is the structured, competitive event that turns intent into offers: define the requirement, invite qualified suppliers, run the right RFx — RFI, RFP, RFQ or e-auction — score fairly, and shortlist for award. It is where strategy meets a supplier's actual price and terms.

Below: the plain concept → how every platform runs an event (fair A vs B vs C) → then we stack process mining, award optimization, AI, orchestration and people on top until you see why standalone is never enough.

L0 · The Concept

What it is

A structured competitive process to go to market: define requirements and a scorecard, identify and qualify suppliers, issue an RFx (RFI/RFP/RFQ) or run an e-auction, evaluate responses, and shortlist for award. It is procurement's market-engagement layer.

Why it exists

Because competition and transparency create value. A disciplined event discovers the market, tests price and capability, protects against bias and fraud, and produces a defensible, auditable selection — before anyone commits to a contract.

What good looks like

Weighted scorecard defined up front · broad but pre-qualified bidder list · the right RFx type for the category · sealed, structured responses · scored on TCO not price alone · cycle time tracked · full audit trail · savings and compliance measured.

L2 · Best Practice

Design principles
  • Spec before supplier — write requirements and a weighted scorecard before you go to market, so the event isn't shaped around an incumbent.
  • Right RFx for the job — RFI to learn the market, RFP for value & complexity, RFQ / e-auction for well-specified commodities.
  • Cast wide, qualify hard — scan the market broadly, then pre-qualify on capability, capacity and risk before inviting.
  • Sealed & auditable — structured responses, controlled communications, and a full audit trail from invite to award.
  • Total value, not just price — score TCO, quality, risk, ESG and innovation, not the sticker number alone.
KPIs & failure modes
  • KPIs — sourcing cycle time, qualified bidders per event, savings identified, % of addressable spend competitively sourced, supplier response rate, first-time-right award.
  • Spec bias — requirements written around the incumbent quietly kill competition before it starts.
  • Too few bidders — a thin, unqualified list means no real price tension.
  • Price-only scoring — awarding on lowest bid ignores risk, quality and TCO, and buys regret.
  • Auction misuse — reverse auctions on relationship-critical or non-commoditized categories damage trust and value.
  • Maverick sourcing — events run outside the process, unrecorded and ungoverned.

Standalone, a sourcing suite runs a clean RFx and a basic auction — but its optimization can't solve complex multi-lot award scenarios, its supplier discovery is limited to its own network, and drafting and scoring stay manual. Best-of-breed optimization (Keelvar, Coupa Sourcing Optimization) solves splits, volume tiers and capacity constraints; discovery tools widen the qualified pool; GenAI drafts and scores responses; process mining exposes the events that never happened. No one tool does all five — the leverage is in wiring them together.

Cheat Sheet — Sourcing & RFx

The 5-second definition

Go to market competitively — define specs, invite qualified suppliers, run RFI/RFP/RFQ or an e-auction, score on TCO, and shortlist for award.

KPIs that matter

Cycle time · qualified bidders per event · savings identified · % competitively sourced · response rate · first-time-right award.

RFx in one line

RFI to learn the market · RFP for value & complexity · RFQ / reverse e-auction for well-specified commodities.

Platforms in one line

Ariba on network + depth · Coupa & Keelvar on award optimization · Jaggaer/Ivalua on configurable RFx · GEP on guided sourcing · Zycus on AI authoring.

The layers

L3 mining finds bypass & bottlenecks · L4 optimization solves complex award + GenAI drafts/scores · L5 intake routes the right path · L6 gives events an owner.

The thesis

No single tool discovers, authors, scores and optimizes award. Value is in the seams.
